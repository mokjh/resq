How it plays

RESQ finds a real table in your room and fits the board to it: wide on a big table, compact on a small one, or a virtual table if there’s none. It stays where you left it next time.

  1. Read the warnings, test an idea

    The card behind the board lists who needs help and what’s coming. Pinch a tool and hover it over a glowing spot: the forecast shows what that placement changes over the next minute. The clock doesn’t start until you press Start.

  2. Place, route, adjust

    Pinch or poke a tool card, then a spot on the board; or pinch, drag and let go. To move people, pick a group and then a destination. For a boat, bus, crawler or winch, pick the vehicle, the group, then the drop-off. Tap a placed pump, generator or sprinkler to switch it.

  3. See what happened

    Results show who reached safety and which goals you met. The debrief names the moment that mattered, and you can try the other plan.

  4. Pause any time

    The Menu control is always on the run controls, and pausing freezes everything. In hands-free play, look at a control until it fills. If your hands leave view mid-rescue, the game pauses and waits.
