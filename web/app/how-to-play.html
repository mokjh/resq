How it plays

RESQ looks for a real table in your room and fits the board to it: a wide board on a big table, a compact one on a small table. No table nearby? Choose the virtual table. Raise, lower or turn it until the near edge is in easy reach; it stays where you left it next time.

  1. Read the warnings, test an idea

    The card behind the board lists who needs help and what’s coming. Pinch a tool and hover it over a glowing spot: the forecast shows what that placement changes over the next minute. The clock doesn’t start until you press Start.

  2. Place, route, adjust

    Pinch or poke a tool card, then a spot on the board; or pinch, drag and let go. To move people, pick a group and then a destination. For a boat, bus, crawler or winch, pick the vehicle, the group, then the drop-off. Tap a placed pump, generator or sprinkler to switch it.

  3. Pause any time

    The Menu control is always on the run controls, and pausing freezes everything. In hands-free play, look at a control until it fills; you can step time forward one or five seconds instead of letting it run. If your hands leave view mid-rescue, the game pauses and waits.
