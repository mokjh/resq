How to play

In development for the Meta VR Start Developer Competition 2026. Not yet released. This guide describes the development build.

Set up

Put the valley on a table

RESQ looks for a real table in your room and fits the board to it: a wide board on a big table, a compact one on a small table. No table nearby? Choose the virtual table. Then raise, lower or turn the board until the near edge is in easy reach. The valley stays where you left it next time.

Plan

Read the warnings, test an idea

The card behind the board lists who needs help and what's coming. Pinch a tool and hover it over a glowing spot: the forecast shows what that placement changes over the next minute. The clock doesn't start until you press Start.

Act

Place, route, adjust

Pinch or poke a tool card, then a spot on the board; or pinch, drag and let go. To move people, pick a group and then a destination. For a boat, bus, crawler or winch, pick the vehicle, the group, then the drop-off. Tap a placed pump, generator or sprinkler to switch it.

Pause

The Menu control is always there

Pausing freezes everything. In hands-free play, look at a control and hold your gaze until it fills; you can also step time forward one or five seconds at a time instead of letting it run. If your hands leave view mid-rescue, the game pauses calmly and waits for you.
