Help

Privacy

RESQ keeps everything on your headset and has no internet access.

  • What stays on the headset
  • Your room
  • Network

What stays on the headset

RESQ saves your progress, settings and any postcards you take in its own storage on the headset. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics.

Your room

If you allow it, RESQ reads where tables are in your room (from your headset’s Space Setup) to place the board, and saves one spatial anchor so the board returns to the same spot. It never records or stores camera images.

Network

The app has no internet permission: it can’t send anything anywhere. This website uses no cookies, trackers or analytics.
