Access

Nobody needs two steady hands to bring everyone home.

These options are in the game’s Settings card and are remembered between sessions.

Settings

  • ControlsTwo handsOne handHands-freeEvery mission finishes with either hand alone, or with no hands: look at a control until it fills.
  • Text size100%125%150%Menus, warnings, the tool tray and the run controls all grow.
  • PaceStandardRelaxedCommandRelaxed slows the hazards. Command steps time forward 1 or 5 seconds at a time.
  • ContrastGlassHigh contrastHigh contrast puts solid panels behind all text.
  • EffectsFullReducedReduced stops flicker and pulsing without hiding any hazard.
  • MissionsIn orderOpen playOpen play unlocks everything. Each world’s first mission is always open.
Hands-free details
Quest 3 and 3S have no eye tracking, so hands-free follows your head, not your eyes. Hold your gaze 0.5 to 1.5 seconds, your choice.
Sound
Warnings, blocked actions and backup calls have their own volume, so they stay clear when music is low. Every warning is also shown as text; there’s no spoken dialogue to miss.
Seated
Designed for seated play at a table. No movement around the room is needed. Restart and leaving a mission always ask first.
