In development · Meta Quest 3 and 3S

Nobody needs two steady hands to bring everyone home.

These options are in Settings and are remembered between sessions.

Either hand alone
Every mission can be finished with one hand, left or right. The run controls can mirror to the left side.
Hands-free
Look at a control and hold your gaze until it fills (0.5 to 1.5 seconds, your choice). Quest 3 and 3S have no eye tracking, so this follows your head, not your eyes.
Your own pace
Relaxed pace slows the hazards. Hands-free play adds +1 s and +5 s steps, so you never have to act in real time. Restart and leaving a mission always ask first.
Text and contrast
Text at 100, 125 or 150 percent; the tool tray and controls grow with it. High contrast puts solid panels behind all text.
Calmer visuals
Reduced effects stops flicker and pulsing without hiding any hazard.
Sound
Warnings, blocked actions and backup calls have their own volume, so they stay clear when music is low. Every warning is also shown as text; there’s no spoken dialogue to miss.
Open play
Unlock every mission from the start. Each world’s first mission is always open.
Seated
Designed for seated play at a table. No movement around the room is needed.

In development for the Meta VR Start Developer Competition 2026. Not yet released. These features are tested automatically in the development build. Testing with people and on headsets happens in November; this page will say what that finds.
