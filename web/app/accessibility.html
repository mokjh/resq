Accessibility

RESQ is built so nobody needs two steady hands, quick reactions or perfect vision to bring everyone home. These options are in Settings and are remembered between sessions.

Either hand aloneEvery mission can be finished with one hand, left or right. The run controls can mirror to the left side.
Hands-freeLook at a control and hold your gaze until it fills (0.5 to 1.5 seconds, your choice). Quest 3 and 3S have no eye tracking, so this follows your head, not your eyes.
Your own paceRelaxed pace slows the hazards. Hands-free play adds +1 s and +5 s steps, so you never have to act in real time. Restart and leaving a mission always ask first.
Text and contrastText at 100, 125 or 150 percent; the tool tray and controls grow with it. High contrast puts solid panels behind all text.
Calmer visualsReduced effects stops flicker and pulsing without hiding any hazard.
SoundWarnings, blocked actions and backup calls use their own volume, so they stay clear when music is low. Every warning is also shown as text; there is no spoken dialogue to miss.
Open playUnlock every mission from the start. Each world's first mission is always open.
SeatedDesigned for seated play at a table. No movement around the room is needed.
