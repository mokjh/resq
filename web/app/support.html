Setting up the board

RESQ asks to use your room's surfaces only so it can place the board on a table. If you say no, or there is no table that fits, choose the virtual table: the missions are the same. To move the board later, open the Menu and choose Move the board.

If the board seems to drift or jump, the headset may have lost track of the room. RESQ pauses and offers to find the table again or switch to the virtual table.

Hands, one hand and hands-free

Pinch with your thumb and index finger, or poke a card with your fingertip. Hands-free: in Settings, set Controls to hands-free; look at a control until it fills. If your hands leave view during a rescue, the game pauses until you press Resume.

Progress and saves

Progress and settings are saved on the headset after every mission. If the save can't be read, RESQ offers your last good backup and never erases anything without asking. Reset progress in Settings clears stars and unlocks but keeps your settings.

Something went wrong

If a mission feels impossible, open the briefing again: it lists who needs help and what's coming. After a failed run, the debrief shows the moment that mattered. Every mission has been won by two different reference plans on both board sizes.
