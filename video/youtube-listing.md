## Description
```
RESQ puts a small valley on a table in front of you. A flood, a forest fire, an avalanche slope, a rising tide or a lava flow is coming, and residents need a way out. Pinch a tool from the tray, place it on a legal spot, and see the forecast before you commit: the real simulation runs ahead and shows what stays dry and what floods.

Five worlds, 25 missions, two winning plans for each, three rule presets and 30 named challenges. Play with two hands, one hand, or head gaze alone. Text scales to 150%, with high-contrast and reduced-effects modes. Start on a virtual table, or put the valley on your own table.

Made for the Meta VR Start Developer Competition 2026 (Gaming track).

Website: https://resq-woad.vercel.app
```
